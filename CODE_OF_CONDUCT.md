# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our open-source quantitative research community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

* Focusing on reproducible, zero-lookahead empirical evidence, mathematical rigor, and clean software engineering.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive statistical and code-review feedback.
* Zero tolerance for harassment, personal attacks, or falsified backtest results.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `yaochristyvan8@gmail.com`.
